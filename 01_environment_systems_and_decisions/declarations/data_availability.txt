The datasets generated and analysed during the current study, together with the benchmark code, the camera recorder, and the Docker configurations, will be deposited in a public repository (Zenodo) with a permanent DOI upon acceptance. The deposit is split into four archives that unpack into a single directory tree: a main archive (~9 MB) holding the code, the per-run measurements for both hosts and all three cameras, the figures, and the manuscript source; and one archive per camera for the 8,724 recorded frames (~1.1 GB in total). No location finer than the country is recorded in any measurement file.
